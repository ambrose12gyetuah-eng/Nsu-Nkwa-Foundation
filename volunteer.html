Volunteer with us

We welcome volunteers year-round for borehole handovers, community hygiene education, school outreach, and data collection. No engineering background required — we train every volunteer before deployment.

  • Weekend community outreach and hygiene talks
  • Assisting Water Committee training sessions
  • Supporting borehole handover events
  • Helping with monitoring and data collection visits
  • Skilled trades: masonry, plumbing, and pump mechanics
Volunteers are notified by email once a placement matching their skills and availability opens up.
