Who we are

A community-first approach to clean water

Founded in 2014 in Sekondi-Takoradi, Nsu Nkwa Foundation partners with rural and peri-urban communities to design water and sanitation solutions that are built, owned, and maintained locally. We don't just install a pump and leave — we train Water and Sanitation Committees to keep it running for decades.

From hand-dug wells in fishing communities to school latrine blocks in farming districts, every project starts with the people who will use it every day.
