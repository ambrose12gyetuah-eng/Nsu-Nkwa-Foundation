Projects & programs

Six ways we're closing the water gap

Adiembra, Sekondi-Takoradi

Adiembra Community Borehole Project

Drilled and mechanised a community borehole, cutting water-collection time from two hours to under ten minutes.

Completed · 1,200 people served

Effia-Kwesimintsim

Safe Sanitation for Schools Initiative

Constructing gender-separated latrines and handwashing stations across eight basic schools.

Ongoing · 8 schools reached

Anlo Beach

Anlo Beach Waste & Water Clean-Up

Monthly clean-ups and a plastic-collection cooperative that protects nearby wells from contamination.

Ongoing

Ahanta West District

Rural Handpump Rehabilitation Programme

Repaired and restored 26 broken handpumps and trained local mechanics to service them going forward.

Completed · 26 pumps restored

Shama District

WASH Education for Girls

Menstrual hygiene education and dignity kits paired with new sanitation facilities in five schools.

Ongoing

Butre

Community Latrine Construction Project

Built household latrines for 340 families, ending open defecation in the community.

Completed · 340 households

Nsu Nkwa Foundation

Bringing clean water and dignified sanitation to communities across coastal Ghana, one borehole at a time.
