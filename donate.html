Support us

Your donation, in real impact

Why your support matters

A single borehole costs less than the price of the trucked water a community would otherwise buy over three years — and it lasts for decades. Every donation goes directly toward drilling, sanitation construction, and the training that keeps these systems running long after we leave.

  • GHS 50 provides hygiene education materials for one classroom
  • GHS 200 supplies handwashing stations for a school block
  • GHS 500 funds handpump spare parts for a rural community
  • GHS 1,000+ contributes directly toward drilling a new borehole

Other ways to give

Prefer bank transfer or mobile money? Contact our finance office directly at donate@nsunkwa.org and we'll send you our donation details along with a receipt for your records.

Nsu Nkwa Foundation is a registered non-profit; all donations go toward active field programmes and are acknowledged in our annual impact report.
