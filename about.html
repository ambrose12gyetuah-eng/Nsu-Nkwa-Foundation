About us

Why Nsu Nkwa Foundation exists

Our History

Nsu Nkwa Foundation began in 2014 when a small group of engineers and public health workers in Sekondi-Takoradi grew frustrated watching donated boreholes fall into disrepair within a year of installation. We set out to do things differently: train communities first, build second.

Our Vision

A Western Region where every household has safe water and dignified sanitation within a five-minute walk of home.

Our Mission

To design, build, and hand over water and sanitation infrastructure that communities can operate and maintain themselves, backed by training, spare parts access, and ongoing technical support.

Core Values

  • Community Ownership
  • Transparency
  • Sustainability
  • Equity & Inclusion
  • Local Partnership
  • Accountability

Our Objectives

  • Provide reliable access to clean water for underserved coastal and rural communities
  • Improve sanitation facilities in schools and public spaces
  • Train local Water and Sanitation Committees for long-term maintenance
  • Raise awareness on hygiene practices that reduce waterborne disease
  • Advocate for stronger community involvement in local water policy
Why we exist: Nearly one in three households in parts of the Western Region still rely on unprotected water sources. We exist to close that gap, one community-owned borehole at a time.
